WHO WE ARE

A Legacy Built on Trust

Welcome to Shahzad Marble, a marble business owned by Mohammad Shahzad.

The business was established in 1992 and has been built around the values of quality, reliability and customer satisfaction.

We understand that marble is more than just a building material. It adds character, elegance and lasting beauty to a space.

Our aim is to help customers find the right marble for their homes, offices, hotels, commercial projects and other spaces.
